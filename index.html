Privacy Policy

Metamorf for iPhone · Effective 21 November 2026

Metamorf reads your workouts and heart data from Apple Health to show you how your training is going, and can save the effort rating and voice note you give a workout. All of it stays on your iPhone and in your own Apple Health. We don't collect it, we don't have servers that receive it, and we never sell it or use it for advertising. To learn which parts of the app get used, Metamorf sends anonymous usage events (never Health data), which you can turn off in Profile.

Who we are

Metamorf is a running and training app for iPhone. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the team that makes Metamorf. You can reach us at sumanth@deepcraft.com.

What Metamorf reads from Apple Health

Only with your permission, which you give in the Apple Health prompt, Metamorf reads:

  • Workouts and their routes.
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV) and VO2max.
  • Distance, steps, active energy, and running and cycling power.
  • Your date of birth and biological sex, which help estimate your maximum heart rate.

It also reads workout effort ratings already in Health, for example ones you gave on your Apple Watch.

What Metamorf saves to Apple Health

Only if you allow it, and only when you save one, Metamorf writes one thing to Apple Health: your effort rating (1 to 10) for a workout, linked to that workout. The note you recorded for the workout is saved with that rating, so both come back if you reinstall Metamorf or move to a new iPhone. Metamorf never changes or deletes your workouts or any other Health data. It only deletes effort ratings it saved itself, when you delete them in the app.

If you don't allow this, your ratings and notes are kept only in Metamorf on your iPhone.

Voice notes

When you record a voice note about a workout, Metamorf uses the microphone only while you're recording. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone with Apple's on-device speech recognition; it isn't sent to us or to any server, and the audio isn't kept. Only the text is saved. On recent versions of iOS, your iPhone may download Apple's speech model the first time; that download doesn't include anything you said.

How it's used

Everything is worked out on your iPhone: training load, readiness, heart-rate zones, fitness estimates and the story of each workout. Your Health data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What's stored on your iPhone

Metamorf keeps a few things on your device so it opens quickly and remembers your choices:

  • Your settings: the first name you enter, your heart-rate settings, your appearance choice and any goal race.
  • Results it has already worked out from your Health data, so it doesn't redo them every time.
  • Your effort ratings and notes, also kept here so they show without waiting for Health.

This stays in the app's storage on your iPhone. Deleting Metamorf deletes it. If you use iCloud or encrypted device backups, Apple may include this storage in your backup, under Apple's terms.

Sharing a workout

When you share a workout, Metamorf draws the image or video on your iPhone and hands it to the app you pick in the share sheet (for example Instagram or Messages). It only includes what you can see in the preview, and your note only if you turn it on. We never receive it. If you choose Save to Photos, Metamorf asks for permission to add it to your photo library. If you put the workout on your own photo, you pick it in Apple's photo picker: Metamorf only sees that one photo, uses it to draw the card on your iPhone and doesn't keep it.

Reminders after a workout

If you turn on "How did it feel?" reminders, Apple Health tells Metamorf when a new workout arrives, and your iPhone shows a notification asking how it went. These notifications are created on your iPhone; nothing is sent through a server, and we don't use push notifications. You can turn them off in Metamorf's Profile page or in Settings at any time.

Recaps, posters and "Ask your runs"

Weekly, monthly and yearly recaps, milestones and the route poster are all worked out and drawn on your iPhone from your Health data and your journal. "Ask your runs" uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model: your question and a summary of your recent training are handled on your iPhone and are not sent to us, to Apple's servers or to anyone else.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn which parts of Metamorf get used and where people get stuck, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, an analytics service, which stores them in the European Union (PostHog EU Cloud, Frankfurt). An event says what happened in the app, for example "the Journal was opened", "an effort rating was saved", "the coach was asked a question" or "onboarding was finished", along with:

  • A random identifier created for this install. It isn't your name, email, Apple ID or any advertising identifier, and it isn't linked to who you are.
  • How the app is set up: whether Apple Health is connected, whether "How did it feel?" reminders are on, units and appearance.
  • Basic device and app details: app version, iOS version, iPhone model, language and approximate country and city, which PostHog works out from your IP address as with any internet connection.

Usage events never include your Health data, workouts, routes, heart rate, effort numbers, notes, voice recordings, the questions you ask the coach or its answers, your name or your goal race. We don't use them for advertising, don't sell them, and don't combine them with data from other apps or websites. We keep them for up to two years.

You can turn this off at any time in Profile › Preferences › Share anonymous usage. When it's off, Metamorf sends nothing.

Maps

To draw the map behind a workout's route, Metamorf asks Apple Maps for map images of that area. Apple receives the map region being requested, as with any app that shows Apple Maps. This is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Metamorf doesn't use your current location.

What we don't do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing of it with anyone beyond the anonymous usage events described above.
  • No crash-reporting service and no session recording.
  • Health data is never sent anywhere.

Your choices

  • You can turn Health permissions on or off at any time. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Metamorf.
  • You can change or clear your settings in Metamorf's Profile page.
  • You can turn off anonymous usage analytics in Profile › Preferences.
  • You can edit or delete any effort rating and note from its workout in Metamorf. Deleting it there also removes it from Apple Health.
  • You can turn off the microphone or speech recognition for Metamorf in Settings at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes everything Metamorf stored on your iPhone. Effort ratings and notes it saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Health information

Metamorf is for fitness guidance, not medical advice. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor before changing how you train if you have a health condition.

Children

Metamorf isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Metamorf handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change ships in the app.
