Privacy Policy

Metamorf for iPhone · Effective 18 November 2026

Metamorf reads your workouts and heart data from Apple Health to show you how your training is going. That data stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, we don't have servers that receive it, and we never sell it or use it for advertising.

Who we are

Metamorf is a running and training app for iPhone. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the team that makes Metamorf. You can reach us at sumanth@deepcraft.com.

What Metamorf reads from Apple Health

Only with your permission, which you give in the Apple Health prompt, Metamorf reads:

  • Workouts and their routes.
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV) and VO2max.
  • Distance, steps, active energy, and running and cycling power.
  • Your date of birth and biological sex, which help estimate your maximum heart rate.

Metamorf only reads from Apple Health. It never writes to or changes your Health data.

How it's used

Everything is worked out on your iPhone: training load, readiness, heart-rate zones, fitness estimates and the story of each workout. Your Health data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What's stored on your iPhone

Metamorf keeps a few things on your device so it opens quickly and remembers your choices:

  • Your settings: the first name you enter, your heart-rate settings, your appearance choice and any goal race.
  • Results it has already worked out from your Health data, so it doesn't redo them every time.

This stays in the app's storage on your iPhone. Deleting Metamorf deletes it. If you use iCloud or encrypted device backups, Apple may include this storage in your backup, under Apple's terms.

Maps

To draw the map behind a workout's route, Metamorf asks Apple Maps for map images of that area. Apple receives the map region being requested, as with any app that shows Apple Maps. This is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Metamorf doesn't use your current location.

What we don't do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services in the app today. If that changes, we'll update this policy first and say what's collected and why. Health data will never be part of it.

Your choices

  • You can turn Health permissions on or off at any time. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Metamorf.
  • You can change or clear your settings in Metamorf's Profile page.
  • Deleting the app removes everything Metamorf stored on your iPhone.

Health information

Metamorf is for fitness guidance, not medical advice. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor before changing how you train if you have a health condition.

Children

Metamorf isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Metamorf handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change ships in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email sumanth@deepcraft.com.
